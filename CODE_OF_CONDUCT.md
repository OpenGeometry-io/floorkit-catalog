# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the floorkit-catalog community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces of this repository, including issues, pull requests, reviews and commit messages. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainers ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#maintainers)) are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with it, and will say why when appropriate.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately, through the route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#how-to-report), and say that it is a conduct report. Do not report it in a public issue. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Consequences

| Impact | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Inappropriate language, or other behaviour deemed unprofessional or unwelcome | A private, written warning that explains what was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested |
| A violation through a single incident or a series of actions | A warning, and no interaction with the people involved for a specified period. Breaking these terms may lead to a ban |
| A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour | A temporary ban from interaction with the community |
| A pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals | A permanent ban |
